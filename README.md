# [CamTols13T]

An LSPosed module that adds new **3x** and **5x** zoom buttons and enables **smoother zoom animation** in the HyperOS 2 & 3 Camera app.

## ✨ Features
- Adds **3x** and **5x** zoom buttons to the HyperOS Camera UI.
- Smoother zoom transition/animation.
- Works system-wide on the stock Camera app (`com.android.camera`).
- Lightweight, no extra UI, and does not interfere with other camera features.

## 📱 Compatibility
- **HyperOS 2**
- **HyperOS 3**
- Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO devices with the stock Camera app.
- May require a specific Camera app version. If buttons don't appear, try updating or adjusting the Camera version.

## ⚙️ Requirements
- Rooted device.
- **LSPosed** (Zygisk) installed and active.
- **HyperOS Camera** app (`com.android.camera`).

## 🚀 Installation
1. Install the module APK.
2. Open **LSPosed Manager**.
3. Enable the module.
4. In **Scope**, check the **Camera** app (`com.android.camera`).
5. Reboot your device.
6. Open the Camera app and check for 3x & 5x zoom buttons.

## ⚠️ Notes
